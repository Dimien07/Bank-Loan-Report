# Bank Loan Portfolio Analysis & Risk Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Bank Loan Portfolio Analysis & Risk Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze loan portfolio performance, credit risk, borrower characteristics, and lending trends.

The objective was to transform loan-level data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that enables stakeholders to monitor key financial KPIs and identify potential risk patterns.

## Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key questions such as:

- How many loan applications has the bank received?
- How much has been funded?
- How much has been received from borrowers?
- What percentage of loans are classified as good or bad?
- Which states generate the most loan applications?
- What are the most common loan purposes?
- Which loan terms are most popular?
- How does loan performance vary by employment length?
- What is the relationship between loan status, interest rate, and DTI?

## Key KPIs

- Total Loan Applications: 38.6K
- Total Funded Amount: $435.8M
- Total Amount Received: $473.1M
- Average Interest Rate: 12.05%
- Average DTI: 13.33%
- Good Loan Applications: 33.2K
- Good Loan Percentage: 86.18%
- Bad Loan Applications: 5.3K
- Bad Loan Percentage: 13.82%

## Key Insights

1. 86.18% of loan applications were classified as good loans.
2. 13.82% of loan applications were classified as bad loans.
3. The bank funded approximately $435.8M in loans.
4. Total amount received was approximately $473.1M.
5. Loan applications increased from approximately 2.3K in January to 4.3K in December.
6. Debt consolidation was one of the largest loan purposes.
7. 36-month loans represented the majority of applications compared with 60-month loans.
8. Charged-off loans had a higher average interest rate than fully paid loans.
9. The dashboard enables geographic analysis of loan applications across U.S. states.
